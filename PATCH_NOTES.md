# Patch notes

## 1.0.0 — The Self-Running Farm

A new Sprinkler that waters farmland from hidden water and speeds crops up; a rebuilt Farming Crate that harvests,
replants and stores a 9×9 field without ever losing items; hoe sweeps (3×3 to 7×7); right-click harvest for every
vanilla crop including nether wart and cocoa, replanting with a seed from the drops and never touching the block
above; no trampling by anyone; harvest indicators as a built-in resource pack; a commented config with 0.5.0
migration; no JaUML dependency. Available for Minecraft 1.20.1 (Fabric, Forge), 1.21.1, 26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3
(Fabric, NeoForge). Crates keep their ids and contents.

Full notes: [patchnotes/1.0.0.md](patchnotes/1.0.0.md).
