=============================================
## Changelog 0.2.0
=============================================

- The mod **now requires `jauml`** to function correctly.
- **New Config Files**:
  - `betterfarming/global`: Allows you to **whitelist hoes** that can use the **right-click to harvest** or the **farmland trampling protection** feature.

- You can now **toggle farmland trampling** when holding a hoe.
- Resolved a bug affecting **right-click harvesting** in **Minecraft 1.18.2**.
